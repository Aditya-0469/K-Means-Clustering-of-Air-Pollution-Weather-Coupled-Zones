Spatial Analysis

Geographic Pattern of Pollution Zones

🏭

Industrial Belt Pattern

Cluster 1 stations form a connected arc: Ghaziabad Loni → Anand Vihar → Okhla → Faridabad → Manesar. This NE-to-SW industrial belt aligns with known emission hotspots.

🌿

Green Corridor Pattern

Cluster 0 includes IT parks (Noida Sec 62, Gurugram Sec 51), residential zones (RK Puram, Dwarka), and outer NCR towns (Sonepat, Greater Noida) — lower emission areas.

💨

Wind Corridor Effect

Prevailing NW winter winds channel pollutants from Haryana/Punjab stubble burning through the Cluster 1 industrial belt, explaining the amplified winter PM2.5 concentrations.
