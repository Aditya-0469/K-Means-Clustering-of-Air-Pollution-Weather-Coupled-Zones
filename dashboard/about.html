Project Overview

This project applies unsupervised K-Means clustering to segment the Delhi National Capital Region (NCR) into naturally occurring pollution-weather behaviour zones by coupling air quality observations (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃) with meteorological data (temperature, wind speed, relative humidity, barometric pressure, visibility) from 18 CPCB CAAQMS stations over a 4-year period (2021–2024).

The identified zones — validated against GRAP pollution episode records — reveal that the NCR consists of two fundamentally distinct pollution regimes: a broad all-season zone (Cluster 0) and a winter severe industrial hotspot zone (Cluster 1) that consistently aligns with GRAP Stage IV activation criteria.

This zone segmentation provides the scientific foundation for proactive, geographically-targeted GRAP enforcement and cluster-conditioned AQI forecasting models, addressing a critical gap in the current one-size-fits-all policy approach.
